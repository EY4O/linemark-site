Bookmarks on lines.

Linemark is a bookmark manager for Chrome. It keeps your bookmarks on colour-coded lines, finds any page by the words inside it, and checks for broken links. All of it lives in your browser.

Save in one gesture

Saving takes one click. Linemark keeps the page's title, description and a cover image, stored on your computer. Filing can wait: new saves go to Unsorted until you put them on a line.

Toolbar button
Saves the page you're on, then lets you pick a line, add tags or a note.
Keyboard
Alt + Shift + S saves it without touching the mouse. Alt + Shift + L opens your library.
Right-click
Save to Linemark works on any page, and on any link inside one.
All open tabs
Save a whole window of research in one go.
Chrome bookmarks
Import them in one click. Folders become lines.

Find anything again

You remember what a page said, not what it was called. Linemark searches the text of the pages you save, and shows you the passage that matched.

Full text
Titles, tags, notes and the words on the page itself.
Filters
#tagsite:type:videois:favis:broken
Address bar
Type lm (lower-case L, M), then Space, to search Linemark from Chrome's address bar.
Four views
Cards, list, compact headlines and a moodboard, with a size slider.
Line icons
Give each line one of 240 built-in icons, or any emoji.

Keep links healthy

Pages move and disappear. Linemark checks your links and sorts out the ones that need attention.

Link check
DeadMovedUnverified Every link sorted, so you only fix what's really broken.
Moved pages
Update a bookmark to its new address in one click.
Duplicates
Finds the same page saved twice and merges tags and notes into the copy it keeps.
Trash
Nothing is deleted straight away. Undo, or restore from Trash.

Private by default

Linemark is local by default. There is no Linemark account and no Linemark server, so there is nothing for anyone to collect.

Your library
Stored in your browser profile, on your computer. Covers are downloaded once and kept there too.
Accounts and tracking
None. No sign-up, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting.
Going online
Only when you ask: saving a page, checking links or fetching previews. Linemark then talks to those websites directly.
Sync
Off unless you turn it on. Then your library is encrypted on your device with your passphrase before it goes to your own Google Drive.
Leaving
Export to HTML, which Chrome can import, or take a full backup, any time.

Your Google account

Linemark only asks for your Google account if you turn on sync, so your bookmarks can follow you to other computers. Everything else works without it.

Linemark asks for one permission, to see, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

This is a private app folder that only Linemark can use. It isn't visible in your Drive, and Linemark can't see any of your other files.

What it stores there

A few files holding your library, encrypted on your computer with a key made from your passphrase (AES-256-GCM) before they're uploaded. Google only ever holds scrambled data, and your passphrase never leaves your device.

What it doesn't do

Linemark has no server. The sign-in token stays in your browser, and nothing is sent to the developer. Your data isn't sold, shared, or used for ads or anything other than syncing your own library.

How to stop

In Linemark, Settings → Sync → Delete cloud copy removes every Linemark file from your Drive. You can also remove Linemark's access at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Linemark's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The full details are in the privacy policy.
